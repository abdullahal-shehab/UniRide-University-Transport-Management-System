Everything You Need for Campus Transport

12 comprehensive modules designed for university transportation management

Student & Faculty Management

Complete user lifecycle — registration, profiles, roles, booking history, and account status management.

Bus Booking & Seat Management

Interactive seat maps, real-time availability, transaction-safe booking, cancellations, and PDF tickets.

Trip & Schedule Management

Create, edit, and reschedule trips with conflict detection, booking windows, and passenger manifests.

Transport Officer Dashboard

Real-time overview: bus count, active trips, occupancy rates, attendance, no-shows, and announcements.

Driver & Helper Management

Profiles, licenses, duty rosters, trip assignments, and conflict detection for transport staff.

Route & Stop Management

CRUD routes and stops, pickup/drop-off points, stop-wise timing, and interactive map visualization.

Live Bus Tracking

Driver GPS updates via Realtime Database, Leaflet maps, bus markers, route visualization, and ETA calculation.

Bus Delay Notifications

Automated delay, cancellation, and route change alerts via in-app notifications and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Transport Analytics

Route demand, occupancy percentages, underutilized buses, and daily/weekly/monthly trends with Chart.js.

Route Subscription

Save preferred routes and stops, subscribe/unsubscribe, view upcoming trips, and receive route-specific alerts.

Reports & Export

Generate PDF and Excel reports for trip occupancy, ridership, duty rosters, attendance, and booking summaries.

Security & Administration

Role-based access, audit logs, booking policies, password reset, backup/restore, and rate limiting.
